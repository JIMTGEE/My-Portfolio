Travel & Consular Services

Believe Travel Consult

Admin dashboard + public self-service portals

Problem

Bookings, offers and a high volume of consular applications were handled manually, with no shared system behind them.

Solution

A unified PHP admin dashboard, plus three standalone public forms — passport, birth certificate and travel pre-screening — each submitting straight into its own record without extra steps for the applicant.

QueensPa School (QPMC)

School management system

Problem

Attendance, fee totals and student progress were tracked inconsistently, and a calculation bug was overstating fee payments.

Solution

Rebuilt daily attendance as a clean present/absent toggle, fixed the fee-totals bug, and built a teacher-facing "Student Diagnosis" view combining grades and conduct into a single On Track / Needs Attention / At Risk flag.
